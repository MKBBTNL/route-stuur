  ★ 42.9 km  [see] The Dam — Edam originated at a dam on the IJe or E that emptied into the Zuiderzee. Around 1230 the sea gates of the Zuiderzee were dammed. At the dam the goods had to be transshipped and tolls could be levied. This enabled Edam to grow into a trading post. Shipbuilding, herring fishing and cheese trading brought Edam to great prosperity.
   43.6 km   Follow signs to junction 98 (exit left, heading S) toward junction 99
  ★ 44.6 km  [see] Sea lock Edam — In 1544 it was decided that Edam's open connection with the Zuiderzee had to be closed to protect the hinterland from the effects of ebb and flow. Edam was strongly opposed to this, because it was feared that the draught in the harbor would be lost. In 1570 the people of Edam destroyed the locks gates. The lock meant the end of Edam's ambitions to once again grow into a major port city.
