  ★ 26.5 km  [see] Waterloo forest — State Monument Waterloopbos is a historic open-air laboratory in a beautiful polder forest. Here hundreds of studies have been carried out into the behavior of water. You will find remnants of the research for the Delta Works, but also water works for exotic places such as the port of Lagos.
